Expanded timeline

The standard timeline shows milestones: releases that changed the field, selected one by one. The expanded timeline adds everything else we know as compact dashed pills — the same timeline with a different level of curation.

  • Milestone: solid pill with logo and date. Fully curated from a primary source.
  • Secondary: a real, reviewed release that does not change the overall landscape — including image, video, audio and tools.
  • Catalog: an automated census from Artificial Analysis, sem curadoria editorial. It covers only text models offered through APIs.

The expanded view is about three times taller. Counterintuitively, zooming in o zoom (+) spreads pills across time and shortens the timeline; zooming out compresses and lengthens it. Data: Artificial Analysis.
