The other three tabs answer what exists, which one to choose and how much it costs. This one answers how. The part of “how” that changes the result most is how much of your computer AI can reach. The same question to the same model produces a very different answer depending on whether it can or cannot open your files and run commands.

Below: the difference in two simple ideas, four work questions answered in both settings, what remains afterwards, what to install, how the measurements were made, and three closing conclusions.

The catalog

Three families: two types of programs that give AI hands, and one family of engines that provide the model. As in section 01, where AI thinks and how far its hands can reach are separate questions. The final panel shows that connecting them now takes one command.
